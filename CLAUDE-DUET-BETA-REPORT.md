# Claude Duet beta notes

I'm beta testing Claude Duet, Anthropic's chief-of-staff style assistant, and I want it to succeed. Over November 10 and 11, 2026 I used it for long working sessions: voice calls and typed chat in the main conversation, two channels, background tasks that open pull requests, and relays to my Claude Code sessions. These notes list the seven reports I sent to Anthropic from inside the app, written as feedback for the team. They are about behaviour (what the assistant says, when it says it, and whether that matches what is happening), not crashes.

All times are UTC on November 11, 2026. I've left out the names of my private projects, repositories and pull requests, and I describe each report in my own neutral wording.

## Reports I sent to Anthropic

| # | Time | What I reported |
|---|------|-----------------|
| 1 | 04:52 | Notifications arrived with no update behind them, and it wasn't clear what "update" meant. Duet would benefit from clearer guidelines for this. |
| 2 | 06:16 | Several background tasks I handed over never reported back to me. |
| 3 | 06:56 | Replies often said "on it" instead of describing what was being done. |
| 4 | 07:00 | Research and task progress should come in small steps rather than one large block. |
| 5 | 10:06 | Work that the assistant could do itself kept coming back to me as steps to take. |
| 6 | 20:07 | Status answers didn't reflect updates that were already visible in the chat. |
| 7 | 20:35 | Relays from the main chat to a channel didn't work. The main assistant couldn't start a task in the channel, and only part of my request arrived. |

I confirmed in the chat that reports 6 and 7 were sent (20:08 and 20:36). Reports 1 to 5 went through the same in-app report card.

## What worked well

Background tasks kept running through call drops and usage resets. Overnight work produced real pull requests with passing checks. A fact-checking step caught a test that had been passing without running (09:15), and at 20:15 a pre-merge check held back two pull requests whose descriptions didn't match their code. When I gave a rule in plain words, it was usually followed from then on. The reports above are about communication and coordination around work that was otherwise getting done.

## How I put this together

I went back through the full main-chat history for November 10 and 11, 2026 and checked each report's time against the chat log. Private project names, repository names and pull request details are removed.
